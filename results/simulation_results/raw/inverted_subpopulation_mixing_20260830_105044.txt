INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Two regulated sub-populations that are transcriptomically opposite
  separate cleanly in UMAP and are bimodal along the group axis - the
  scenario the reviewer raises.

  Under GMP-Cor that mixture does not look dysregulated: the separating
  mode ADDS eigenvalue mass, so the mixture sits ABOVE both pure
  populations, and far above a dysregulated one. The lowest value
  reachable by mixing (the mixture floor, above) is the quantity to
  compare the experimental Dis-Arrest value against.

  Group-mean centering separates the two contributions: it removes the
  between-population mode while leaving within-cell coordination intact.
  dGMP is therefore a direct test on real data of whether an observed GMP-Cor
  is mixture structure or genuine coordination — a test that requires
  single-cell data and cannot be performed on bulk RNA-seq.
