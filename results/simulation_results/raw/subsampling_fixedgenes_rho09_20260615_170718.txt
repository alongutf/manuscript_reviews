INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  With the full 2000-gene panel retained and only cells subsampled, raw
  GMP-Cor still falls as cells are removed under both gene-selection
  rules (top: 36.1 -> 50.9; random: 25.2 -> 38.4 from 200 to 1000
  cells). Fewer cells means noisier correlation estimates and a higher
  Marchenko-Pastur noise edge (aspect ratio rises to 10:1 genes:cells at
  200 cells), so less eigenvalue mass clears the threshold.

  At 200 cells the top-expressed panel still retains 71% of its full-
  size (1000-cell) GMP-Cor, whereas the random panel retains only 66%.
  Keeping all 2000 genes does not rescue the random selection at small
  cell numbers: informative (highly-expressed) genes are what carry the
  detectable correlation structure. The signal is recovered far more
  stably across cell numbers when genes are chosen by expression —
  consistent with restricting analysis to detected / variable genes as
  done for the experimental data.
