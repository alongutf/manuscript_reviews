INTERPRETATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Raw GMP-Cor is an EXTENSIVE quantity: it is a sum of eigenvalue
  excesses, and after standardization the total spectral mass
  equals the number of genes. It therefore scales almost linearly
  with dataset size (per-size means span 131% over the 5x range;
  linear fit GMP-Cor ~ 0.0257 * n_genes + 0.05). Raw GMP-Cor
  values are thus only comparable between datasets of equal
  dimension.

  Normalising to a per-gene (INTENSIVE) index, GMP-Cor / n_genes,
  removes this size dependence: across the same 5x range the per-
  gene index is essentially constant (per-size means span only
  7.4% about their mean of 0.02575). The correlation structure of
  a regulated (rho=0.9) population is recovered at every
  subsample size and is not an artefact of the number of cells
  profiled. GMP-Cor is robust to subsampling once the metric is
  made scale-free.
