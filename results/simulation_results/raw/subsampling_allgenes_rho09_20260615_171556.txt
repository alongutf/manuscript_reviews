INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  Using the complete 2000-gene set and subsampling cells only, raw
  GMP-Cor declines smoothly from 38.9 at 1000 cells to 24.6 at 200
  cells (a 46% spread over the 5x range), retaining 63% of the full-
  size value at 200 cells. The fall is driven by fewer cells giving
  noisier correlation estimates and a higher Marchenko-Pastur noise
  edge (the aspect ratio reaches 10:1 genes:cells at 200 cells), not
  by any loss of genes.

  Variance grows as cells are removed (CV 0.04 at 1000 cells -> 0.45
  at 200 cells), so small-cell estimates are noisier but the mean
  signal remains clearly elevated. With the gene panel held
  complete, GMP-Cor is robust to cell subsampling down to a few
  hundred cells.
