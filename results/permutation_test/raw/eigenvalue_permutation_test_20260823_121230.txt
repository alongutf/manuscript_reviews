p_lambda_1 is PARAMETRIC: a distribution family is fitted by MLE to the B
scrambled values of lambda_1 and p = SF_fitted(lambda_obs_1). Family chosen
by AIC. log10_p_lo/hi bracket the answer across every fitted family: the
lightest tail (normal) gives the smallest p, the heaviest (Gumbel/lognormal)
the largest. Tracy-Widom, the theoretical law for the largest eigenvalue of
a Wishart-type null, has right tail exp(-(2/3)s^1.5) and so lies BETWEEN the
normal exp(-s^2/2) and the Gumbel exp(-s): the band contains the truth.
extrapolated=True marks p < 1/B, where the value follows from the assumed
tail shape rather than from the null sample -- read those as "below what
B=100 draws can resolve", not as the numeral.
p_lambda_1_empirical is the exceedance count, censored at 1/(B+1) = 0.0099.
pit_ks_p is a held-out calibration check of the fit over its OBSERVABLE
range (fit on half the draws, PIT the other half); large = no misfit
detected where data exist. It cannot validate the far tail.
