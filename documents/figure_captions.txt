Figure 2 | Eigenvalue spectrum analysis of gene-gene correlations in bacterial transcriptomic data.
a, Eigenvalue spectrum of a random Gaussian matrix (N = 500 cells, P = 1000 genes, aspect ratio γ = P/N = 2) compared with the analytical Marchenko-Pastur (MP) distribution, confirming that uncorrelated data conform to the MP null model.
b, Generalized Marchenko-Pastur (GMP) distributions for random matrices with underlying correlations parameterized by correlation strength χ (0, 0.7, 0.8, 0.9), illustrating how increasing χ progressively shifts spectral weight toward larger eigenvalues and extends the upper spectral edge.
c, Representative synthetic sparse count matrix (22 cells × 14 genes; top) and its column-scrambled counterpart (bottom), which preserves marginal gene distributions while destroying inter-gene correlations.
d, Eigenvalue density of the synthetic matrix (top) color-coded by region: grey bars below the MP upper edge λ_max^MP (dashed line) indicate noise eigenvalues; salmon bars between λ_max^MP and the scrambled maximum λ_max^scr (dotted line) reflect sparsity-induced correlations; blue bars exceeding λ_max^scr represent biologically meaningful correlations. The scrambled eigenvalue density (bottom) provides the empirical noise ceiling.
e, Eigenvalue density histogram for exponential-phase E. coli (original, coloured; scrambled, black) with an inset complementary cumulative distribution function (CCDF) on log-log axes. The threshold λ_max^scr (dashed line) separates noise (grey) from signal (blue) eigenvalues.
f–g, Log-log CCDF of eigenvalues for exponential-phase E. coli (f) and K. pneumoniae (g) from Ma et al., reproducing the noise/signal separation across independent datasets. In all CCDF panels, grey: noise eigenvalues; blue: signal eigenvalues above λ_max^scr; black: scrambled distribution.

Figure 3 | GMP-Cor captures genome-wide correlation strength and distinguishes regulated from disarrested growth-arrest states.
a, d, Log-log CCDF of correlation eigenvalues for the Regulated-Arrest (a, steelblue) and Disarrested-Arrest (d, coral) experimental conditions. The dashed vertical line marks λ_max^scr; eigenvalues exceeding this threshold (signal, colored) reflect biologically coherent gene-gene correlations absent in the scrambled control (black).
b, e, Corresponding CCDFs from synthetic simulations at correlation strength ρ = 0.9 (b) and ρ = 0.5 (e), qualitatively reproducing the high- and low-signal profiles observed experimentally.
c, f, Gene-gene correlation matrices generated by the hub-structured covariance model at ρ = 0.9 (c) and ρ = 0.5 (f), illustrating the density of pairwise correlations underlying each spectral regime (first 100 genes shown; red: positive, blue: negative correlations).
g, GMP-Cor calibration curve obtained by sweeping ρ from 0 to 1 in synthetic data (n = 50 replicates per ρ value); points show median ± s.d. Reference lines indicate the median GMP-Cor of the Regulated-Arrest (steelblue dashed) and Disarrested-Arrest (coral dashed) experimental groups, anchoring the two biological conditions on the simulation-derived axis.
h, Distribution of GMP-Cor scores across individual samples in the Regulated-Arrest and Disarrested-Arrest groups; individual data points are overlaid with jitter. Horizontal dashed lines reproduce the medians from g. Statistical comparison by two-sided Mann-Whitney U test; asterisks denote significance thresholds (****p < 0.0001, ***p < 0.001, **p < 0.01, *p < 0.05).
