INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Mixing two experimentally distinct populations RAISES GMP-Cor above both
  of the populations being mixed, and group-mean centering removes most of
  the excess -- i.e. the elevation is the between-population mode, not new
  within-cell coordination. Population heterogeneity therefore cannot
  produce a LOW GMP-Cor, which is what the Dis-Arrest interpretation
  requires. This is the experimental counterpart of the simulated
  inverted-sub-population scenario in
  simulations/inverted_subpopulation_mixing_run.py.

  dGMP is the diagnostic to quote: it is the fraction of a mixture GMP-Cor
  that disappears when each population is centered on its own gene means,
  i.e. the fraction that is between-population structure rather than
  within-cell coordination.
